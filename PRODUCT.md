# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People whose names the host has already entered in the Secret Santa database. They use the app on a phone or computer to see the roster and make their one match.

## Product Purpose

The app runs a one-to-one Secret Santa draw for a group. Each participant selects their own name, receives one other person as their match, and is prevented from drawing again in the same browser session. The host manages the roster and can review assignments in the database.

## Brand Personality

Fun, festive, and gift-centered. The interface should feel welcoming and seasonal while keeping the matching task clear.

## Anti-references

Avoid corporate SaaS styling, signup-oriented flows, and cluttered or childish Christmas decoration.

## Design Principles

- Show the current roster clearly before asking participants to act.
- Keep the path from choosing a name to receiving a match direct and understandable.
- Make the one-to-one draw feel festive without obscuring names or actions.
- Treat phones as a primary way to use the app.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use standard accessibility practices: keyboard-operable controls, clear focus states, readable contrast, mobile-friendly sizing, and reduced-motion support.
