# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Hosts create a shareable basket, choose a participant capacity that excludes the host, and invite a group with a short link. Guests join by entering their own name. The host can launch only after every participant place is filled; guests then make one match each.

## Product Purpose

The app lets anyone host a one-to-one Secret Santa draw. Each basket has its own host, capacity, roster, and matching state. Guests join from a shared link, then receive one other person as their match after launch. A participant is prevented from drawing again in the same browser session.

## Brand Personality

Fun, festive, and gift-centered. The interface should feel welcoming and seasonal while keeping the matching task clear.

## Anti-references

Avoid corporate SaaS styling, signup-oriented flows, and cluttered or childish Christmas decoration.

## Design Principles

- Make basket creation and invitation easy on a phone.
- Show the current roster and places remaining before asking participants to act.
- Keep the path from choosing a name to receiving a match direct and understandable.
- Make the one-to-one draw feel festive without obscuring names or actions.
- Treat phones as a primary way to use the app.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use standard accessibility practices: keyboard-operable controls, clear focus states, readable contrast, mobile-friendly sizing, and reduced-motion support.
